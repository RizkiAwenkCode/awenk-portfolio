# Product Specification

## Product

Awenk is a credible, personable portfolio for Awenk, a frontend engineer targeting recruiters and engineering managers at banks, government institutions, and digital-infrastructure organizations.

## Goal

Communicate frontend engineering capability, experience with complex interfaces and data, and a trustworthy working approach without overstating unconfirmed project results.

## Primary audience

- Recruiters evaluating role fit and credibility.
- Engineering managers evaluating frontend depth, collaboration, and delivery judgment.
- Technical stakeholders in regulated, public-sector, financial, and infrastructure contexts.

## Core user outcomes

Visitors should be able to understand who Awenk is, what he builds, where he has worked, which technologies he uses, and how to contact him.

## Product principles

- Specific and factual over impressive-sounding.
- Personable and clear over performative.
- Accessible, responsive, and performance-conscious.
- NDA-safe presentation of work.
- Explicit placeholders wherever facts or assets are missing.

## Scope

The approved information architecture is Global, Hero, Capabilities, Selected Work, Case Study, Process, Experience, Toolbox, About, and Contact. The Case Study is a separate flagship page; the other sections form the homepage experience.

## Constraints

- Indonesian is the default language with an ID/EN toggle.
- The tagline remains English in both languages.
- Mobile layout is not approved and must be proposed separately.
- No deployment or publishing without approval.
- A generated homepage concept image may guide visual direction, but written specifications take precedence. Its invented copyright year and footer tagline must be ignored.

## Success criteria

The portfolio presents verified information clearly, supports recruiter and manager scanning, works with keyboard and assistive technology, maintains WCAG AA contrast, and leaves no invented content in the final experience.
