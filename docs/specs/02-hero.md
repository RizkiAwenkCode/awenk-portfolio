# 02 Hero

## Purpose

Introduce Awenk, establish the Complexity Simplified positioning, and provide immediate routes to work and contact.

## Approved content

Headline/tagline: Complexity Simplified.

Indonesian: Saya mengubah antarmuka kompleks menjadi aplikasi web yang responsif dan mudah digunakan, dengan pengalaman frontend lebih dari 5 tahun.

English: I turn complex interfaces into responsive, user-friendly web applications, with over 5 years of frontend experience.

CTAs: Lihat karya / View work; Hubungi saya / Contact me.

Expertise labels: React & Next.js, Data & GIS, Integrasi API.

## Layout

Split layout on desktop: text left, abstract geometric visual right. A personal photo may be added later; use no portrait until supplied.

## Interactions

Work CTA scrolls to Selected Work. Contact CTA scrolls to Contact. Labels are informational and do not require interaction.

## Accessibility

Use one primary page heading, readable text at minimum 16px, semantic buttons or links, meaningful alternative text for any supplied visual, and a labeled placeholder when visual content is unavailable. Ensure the visual does not carry essential meaning alone.

## Acceptance criteria

- Complexity Simplified is the primary hero message.
- Both approved language variants are available.
- Both CTAs reach their intended sections.
- The three approved expertise labels are shown.
- The right-side visual is abstract or explicitly marked as a placeholder, never an invented portrait.

## Unresolved details

- Abstract geometric visual direction and supplied concept image.
- Exact hero heading scale within approved sizes.
- CTA hierarchy and final translated microcopy.
- Responsive and mobile layout are not approved.

## Implementation status

Specification approved; implementation not started.
