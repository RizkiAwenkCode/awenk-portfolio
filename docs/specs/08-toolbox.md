# 08 Toolbox

## Purpose

Show the breadth of Awenk's working toolkit without implying proficiency ratings or unverified expertise levels.

## Approved content

Groups: Frontend; State & API; Data & GIS; Backend & Automation; AI & Mobile; Workflow.

Starter technology list: React.js, Next.js, Vue.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Redux, Zustand, Axios, REST, PostgreSQL, PostGIS, QGIS, ArcGIS, Node.js, Supabase, Gemini API, Kotlin, Git, Figma, Postman.

The list may expand after the full CV is reviewed. No skill ratings.

## Layout

Compact text groups arranged for quick scanning. Use semantic group headings and avoid decorative skill meters.

## Interactions

Informational only. No rating, sorting, filtering, or progress controls.

## Accessibility

Use headings and lists for groups and technologies. Ensure text remains at least 16px and readable at WCAG AA contrast. Do not rely on visual grouping alone.

## Acceptance criteria

- All six group names appear.
- All confirmed technologies appear without ratings.
- The list is labeled as a starter list in content context.
- Unknown additions are not invented.

## Unresolved details

- Exact technology-to-group assignments.
- Full CV additions and preferred ordering.
- Responsive and mobile layout are not approved.

## Implementation status

Specification approved; implementation not started.
