## Interactions

On first entry into view, pulse a yellow outline twice around the contact links, then stop. Reduced-motion mode uses a static highlight and immediate scrolling. Email, LinkedIn, and phone links use their approved targets. Never imply online status or immediate availability.

## Accessibility

Use a meaningful section heading, semantic links with descriptive accessible names, visible keyboard focus, WCAG AA contrast, and a reduced-motion media query. The highlight must not be the only indication of focus or availability.

## Acceptance criteria

- Both approved headings are available through language switching.
- All three contact routes use the exact approved display data and targets.
- The yellow outline pulses exactly twice on first entry, then stops.
- Reduced motion produces a static highlight and immediate scrolling.
- No online or immediate-availability claim appears.
